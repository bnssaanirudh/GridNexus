# Topology-Aware Dual-Certificate Corrective Settlement for Distributed Energy Transactions

## Abstract
Local energy markets have emerged as a scalable solution for integrating distributed energy resources (DERs). However, most market mechanisms ignore the underlying physical grid topology, leading to dispatch instructions that violate voltage and thermal constraints. To bridge this gap without sacrificing the economic efficiency of peer-to-peer trading, we propose a novel topology-aware corrective settlement framework. It introduces a hierarchical physical verification mechanism, a corrective projection operator that salvages violating trades rather than discarding them, and a dual-certificate mechanism that cryptographically binds economic settlements to verified physical states. Experimental results on standard IEEE distribution networks demonstrate that our framework eliminates physical violations while preserving over 90% of the originally proposed market welfare, fundamentally outperforming reject-only baseline models.
