## Conclusion for Counsel
- The specific use of **state-bound dual certificates** to cryptographically link an approved P2P trade with a verified hierarchical power-flow topology appears absent from the searched literature.
- **NEEDS PROFESSIONAL REVIEW** to verify novelty of the corrective projection step in a decentralized market context.
